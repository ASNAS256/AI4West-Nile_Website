Empowering West Nile Through Artificial Intelligence

A community-driven initiative introducing, democratizing, and applying AI technologies to solve real-world challenges across Agriculture, Health, Education, Trade, and Technology in West Nile, Uganda.

About AI 4 West Nile

West Nile AI Drive (AI 4 West Nile) is a regional innovation movement focused on creating awareness, building AI skills, and developing local AI-driven solutions for communities across West Nile.

We believe Artificial Intelligence can help improve healthcare delivery, agricultural productivity, education systems, business growth, and youth innovation opportunities.

Focus Sectors

Agriculture

AI-powered farming insights, smart irrigation, disease detection, and agricultural data systems.

Health

Digital health systems, maternal healthcare innovations, diagnostics, and health analytics.

Education

AI learning platforms, digital literacy, and youth innovation programs.

Trade & Business

Business intelligence, digital entrepreneurship, and market access solutions.

Information Technology

Software development, AI engineering, cybersecurity, cloud technologies, and digital innovation ecosystems.
